# Community conduct

Treat contributors and users respectfully. Discuss ideas and implementation choices with clear evidence. Harassment, discriminatory abuse, threats, doxxing and publication of private credentials or account data are unacceptable.

Keep reports and reviews constructive. Respect requests to stop unwanted contact. Do not repost private support or security conversations without consent.

The maintainer may edit/remove harmful content, limit participation or close discussions to protect contributors. Apply these standards consistently, including to maintainers.

A dedicated private conduct-reporting contact is pending maintainer setup. Until then use an already established private channel with the maintainer; do not post sensitive personal details in a public issue. See SECURITY.md for vulnerability reporting.
